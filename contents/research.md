## 🔬 Research Areas

### 🤖 Embodied Intelligence

My primary research focus is on **Embodied Intelligence** - the study of intelligent systems that can interact with and learn from the physical world. This interdisciplinary field combines elements of robotics, machine learning, and cognitive science.

#### Current Projects
- **Autonomous Navigation Systems** - Developing AI agents that can navigate complex environments using only visual input
- **Manipulation Learning** - Teaching robots to perform dexterous manipulation tasks through reinforcement learning
- **Sensorimotor Learning** - Creating systems that learn to perceive and act through interaction with their environment

#### Key Contributions
- Developed novel architectures for sensorimotor learning
- Published papers on embodied AI at top-tier conferences
- Open-source contributions to embodied intelligence frameworks

---

### 👁️ Computer Vision

I specialize in **computer vision** with applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and intelligent perception.

#### Research Interests
- **3D Vision** - Depth estimation, point cloud processing, and 3D reconstruction
- **Object Detection & Recognition** - Real-time detection and classification of objects in complex scenes
- **Visual Odometry** - Estimating camera motion from visual input alone
- **Semantic Segmentation** - Pixel-level understanding of visual scenes

#### Applications
- Autonomous vehicle perception systems
- Robotic manipulation and grasping
- Augmented reality and virtual reality
- Medical image analysis
